5-Step Roadmap for Pakistani Applicants

Proven steps recommended by PSAK senior researchers to secure admission in Korea.

STEP 01

Target Labs

Search faculty directories for labs matching your BS/MS thesis background.

STEP 02

Draft Cold Email

Write concise email (< 250 words) attaching your academic CV and transcript.

STEP 03

PI Interview

Attend Zoom interview with professor to discuss research goals and stipend.

STEP 04

Apply to Portal

Submit formal application with HEC/MOFA-attested degrees and IELTS/TOEFL.

STEP 05

Visa & Arrival

Receive Certificate of Admission (CoA), apply for D-2 visa, and travel to Korea.
