About PSAK & Executive Cabinet

A Home Away From Home for Pakistani Scholars

Pakistan Students and Scholars Association Korea (PSAK) is an independent non-profit organization established to provide a supportive collective voice for Pakistani students, graduate researchers, postdoctoral fellows, and visiting professors residing throughout the Republic of Korea.

Whether assisting prospective applicants with the Global Korea Scholarship (GKS), welcoming newly arrived master’s and doctoral candidates, or organizing nationwide sports tournaments and cultural galas, PSAK actively builds bridges between the Pakistani scholarly community and Korean society.

Our Mission

To facilitate academic progress, cultural integration, and community welfare for all Pakistani scholars in Korea.

Our Vision

To produce globally competitive researchers and strengthen academic cooperation between Pakistan and South Korea.
